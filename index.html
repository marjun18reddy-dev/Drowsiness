SECTION 04

Data Preprocessing & Transformations

Custom aspect ratio padding, data augmentations, and PyTorch DataLoader specifications.

🔄 Transformation Pipeline
  • 1
    SquarePadResize(227)

    Aspect-ratio preserving black border padding fill=(0,0,0) followed by bilinear resize to 227×227.

  • 2
    Data Augmentation (Train Only)

    RandomHorizontalFlip(p=0.5), RandomRotation(degrees=10), ColorJitter(brightness=0.2, contrast=0.2).

  • 3
    ToTensor & Normalization

    Scales values to [0.0, 1.0] and normalizes with mean=[0.5, 0.5, 0.5], std=[0.5, 0.5, 0.5].

SECTION 05

DrowsinessCNN Architecture

Custom 3-block convolutional neural network with adaptive pooling and dense classifier.

INPUT 3 × 227 × 227
↓
Block 1
Conv2D (3 → 32, 3×3, p=1) BatchNorm2d(32) + ReLU MaxPool2d (2×2, s=2)
Out: 32 × 113 × 113
↓
Block 2
Conv2D (32 → 64, 3×3, p=1) BatchNorm2d(64) + ReLU MaxPool2d (2×2, s=2)
Out: 64 × 56 × 56
↓
Block 3
Conv2D (64 → 128, 3×3, p=1) BatchNorm2d(128) + ReLU MaxPool2d (2×2, s=2)
Out: 128 × 28 × 28
↓
Adaptive Avg Pool
Out: 128 × 1 × 1
↓
Classifier
Flatten → 128 Linear (128 → 64) + ReLU Dropout (p=0.5) Linear (64 → 2)
Out: 2 Logits
